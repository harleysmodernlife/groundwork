## Solving Two-Step Equations

A two-step equation requires exactly two inverse operations to isolate the variable. This is the natural progression from one-step equations — instead of one thing happening to the variable, two things are happening, and you undo them in reverse order. Understanding this process prepares you for solving every more-complex equation in algebra.

---

## The Logic: Reverse Order of Operations

When an expression like 2x + 5 is built, two operations were applied to x in this order:
1. Multiply x by 2 (getting 2x)
2. Add 5 (getting 2x + 5)

To solve 2x + 5 = 11, you undo those operations in **reverse** order:
1. First, undo the addition: subtract 5 from both sides.
2. Then, undo the multiplication: divide both sides by 2.

This reversal of order is the key principle. You peel away operations starting from the outermost (addition/subtraction) before addressing the inner ones (multiplication/division).

---

## General Procedure

1. **Isolate the variable term:** Use addition or subtraction to move any constant to the other side.
2. **Solve for the variable:** Use multiplication or division to remove the coefficient.
3. **Check:** Substitute your answer back into the original equation.

---

## Example 1: Basic Two-Step

Solve: 3x + 7 = 22

Step 1 — Remove the constant (subtract 7 from both sides):
- 3x + 7 - 7 = 22 - 7
- 3x = 15

Step 2 — Remove the coefficient (divide both sides by 3):
- x = 15 ÷ 3
- x = **5**

Check: 3(5) + 7 = 15 + 7 = 22. ✓

---

## Example 2: Subtraction in the Equation

Solve: 4n - 9 = 27

Step 1 — Add 9 to both sides:
- 4n = 36

Step 2 — Divide both sides by 4:
- n = **9**

Check: 4(9) - 9 = 36 - 9 = 27. ✓

---

## Example 3: Negative Coefficient

Solve: -2x + 11 = 3

Step 1 — Subtract 11 from both sides:
- -2x = -8

Step 2 — Divide both sides by -2:
- x = **4**

Check: -2(4) + 11 = -8 + 11 = 3. ✓

---

## Example 4: Variable on Right Side

The variable doesn't have to be on the left. The algebra works the same way.

Solve: 16 = 5m - 4

Step 1 — Add 4 to both sides:
- 20 = 5m

Step 2 — Divide both sides by 5:
- 4 = m, or m = **4**

Check: 5(4) - 4 = 20 - 4 = 16. ✓

---

## Example 5: Fractional Results

Solve: 6x + 5 = 14

Step 1: 6x = 9
Step 2: x = 9/6 = **3/2** or 1.5

Check: 6(1.5) + 5 = 9 + 5 = 14. ✓

Answers don't always come out to whole numbers. Leave as a fraction or decimal as appropriate.

---

## Example 6: Equation with Division

Solve: x/3 - 4 = 8

Step 1 — Add 4 to both sides:
- x/3 = 12

Step 2 — Multiply both sides by 3:
- x = **36**

Check: 36/3 - 4 = 12 - 4 = 8. ✓

---

## Equations with Parentheses

If there are parentheses, use the distributive property first to clear them, then solve.

Solve: 2(x + 3) = 14

Distribute: 2x + 6 = 14
Step 1: 2x = 8
Step 2: x = **4**

Check: 2(4 + 3) = 2(7) = 14. ✓

## Translating Word Problems

**Example:** "Five more than three times a number is 26. Find the number."
- "Three times a number" → 3n
- "Five more than" → 3n + 5
- "is 26" → = 26
- Equation: 3n + 5 = 26
- 3n = 21 → n = **7**

**Example:** "A plumber charges $85 for a service call plus $65 per hour. A customer's total bill was $345. How many hours did the job take?"
- Total = 85 + 65h = 345
- 65h = 260
- h = **4 hours**

**Example:** "After doubling your savings and spending $30, you have $170. What did you start with?"
- 2s - 30 = 170
- 2s = 200
- s = **$100**

---

## Common Mistakes

**Mistake 1:** Dividing before subtracting. In 2x + 6 = 10, don't divide by 2 first — the +6 is outside the multiplication and must go first.

**Mistake 2:** Not applying subtraction to both sides. 3x + 4 = 19: wrong is 3x = 19 - 4 without showing it applies to both sides. Right is: 3x + 4 - 4 = 19 - 4.

**Mistake 3:** Sign errors in checks. If you compute the check and it doesn't match, redo the check carefully before assuming your algebra is wrong. It's often a sign error in the check itself.

## Practice

Solve and check:

1. 5x + 3 = 23
2. 7n - 12 = 44
3. -4y + 9 = -23
4. m/6 + 2 = 9
5. 3(x - 4) = 21
6. A car rental company charges $40 per day plus a $25 insurance fee. A renter paid $185. How many days did they rent?
7. After tripling his hourly rate and subtracting $2 for a fee, a contractor nets $46 per hour. What was his original rate?

**Answers:**
1. 5x = 20 → x = **4**
2. 7n = 56 → n = **8**
3. -4y = -32 → y = **8**
4. m/6 = 7 → m = **42**
5. 3x - 12 = 21 → 3x = 33 → x = **11**
6. 40d + 25 = 185 → 40d = 160 → d = **4 days**
7. 3r - 2 = 46 → 3r = 48 → r = **$16 per hour**
