## Perimeter and Area

Perimeter and area answer two different questions about shapes: perimeter tells you the total length of a shape's boundary, and area tells you how much surface the shape covers. These concepts are foundational for any work involving physical space — construction, landscaping, carpeting a room, fencing a yard, painting a wall.

---

## Perimeter: Distance Around a Shape

Perimeter is measured in **linear units**: inches, feet, meters, centimeters, etc.

The perimeter of any polygon (closed shape with straight sides) is the sum of all its side lengths.

### Rectangle

A rectangle has two lengths and two widths. Its perimeter formula:

```
P = 2l + 2w   or equivalently   P = 2(l + w)
```

**Example:** A rectangle is 12 feet long and 7 feet wide.
- P = 2(12) + 2(7) = 24 + 14 = **38 feet**

### Square

All four sides of a square are equal, so:

```
P = 4s
```

**Example:** A square lot with side 45 feet.
- P = 4(45) = **180 feet**

### Triangle

P = a + b + c (sum of the three side lengths)

**Example:** A triangle with sides 8, 10, and 14 inches.
- P = 8 + 10 + 14 = **32 inches**

### Irregular Polygons

Add all sides regardless of shape. A hexagonal room with sides 5, 7, 9, 6, 4, and 8 feet:
- P = 5 + 7 + 9 + 6 + 4 + 8 = **39 feet**

## Area: Surface Coverage

Area is measured in **square units**: square inches (in²), square feet (ft²), square meters (m²), etc. The "squared" unit reflects that you're measuring in two dimensions simultaneously.

### Rectangle

```
A = l × w
```

**Example:** A room is 15 feet by 12 feet. How much flooring is needed?
- A = 15 × 12 = **180 ft²**

### Square

```
A = s²
```

**Example:** A square tile 9 inches on each side.
- A = 9² = **81 in²**

### Triangle

The area of a triangle is half the area of a rectangle with the same base and height:

```
A = (1/2) × base × height
```

The height must be **perpendicular** to the base — straight up, not along a slanted side.

**Example:** A triangular garden with base 14 meters and height 9 meters.
- A = (1/2)(14)(9) = (1/2)(126) = **63 m²**

### Parallelogram

```
A = base × height
```

Again, height is the perpendicular distance between the parallel sides, not the slant side.

**Example:** Parallelogram with base 10 cm and height 6 cm.
- A = 10 × 6 = **60 cm²**

### Trapezoid

A trapezoid has two parallel sides called bases (b₁ and b₂) with different lengths, plus a height.

```
A = (1/2)(b₁ + b₂) × height
```

**Example:** Trapezoid with bases 8 ft and 14 ft, height 5 ft.
- A = (1/2)(8 + 14)(5) = (1/2)(22)(5) = **55 ft²**

## Circle: Circumference and Area

A circle doesn't have "sides," so its boundary (circumference) uses a different formula. Pi (π ≈ 3.14159 or use 3.14 for estimates) is the ratio of circumference to diameter — constant for all circles.

**Circumference (perimeter of a circle):**
```
C = πd   or   C = 2πr
```
Where d = diameter (distance across the center) and r = radius (distance from center to edge). Note: d = 2r.

**Area of a circle:**
```
A = πr²
```

**Example:** A circular fountain with diameter 10 feet.
- Radius = 5 feet
- Circumference = π × 10 ≈ 3.14 × 10 = **31.4 feet**
- Area = π × 5² = π × 25 ≈ 3.14 × 25 = **78.5 ft²**

## Composite Figures

Real shapes are often combinations of simpler shapes. Split them into recognizable pieces, find each area, then add or subtract.

**Example:** An L-shaped room:
```
+--------+
|  6×4   |
+----+   |
     | 3×8|
     +----+
```
- Upper rectangle: 6 × 4 = 24 ft²
- Lower rectangle: 3 × 8 = 24 ft²
- Total area = **48 ft²**

**Example:** A rectangular window with a semicircular top arch:
- Rectangle: 3 ft wide × 4 ft tall = 12 ft²
- Semicircle: radius = 1.5 ft; area = (1/2)πr² = (1/2)(3.14)(2.25) ≈ 3.53 ft²
- Total ≈ **15.53 ft²**

## Real-World Applications

**Flooring:** A room 18 ft × 24 ft needs carpet. Area = 432 ft². If carpet costs $3.50/ft², total cost = 432 × $3.50 = $1,512.

**Fencing:** A yard is 60 ft × 40 ft. One side (60 ft) borders a house. Fence needed: 60 + 40 + 40 = **140 ft** (three sides only).

**Paint:** A wall is 12 ft × 9 ft with one window 3 ft × 4 ft.
- Wall area: 12 × 9 = 108 ft²
- Window: 3 × 4 = 12 ft² (don't paint this)
- Paintable area: 108 - 12 = **96 ft²**

## Practice

1. Find the perimeter and area of a rectangle 9 m long and 5 m wide.
2. A square room has perimeter 60 feet. What is the area?
3. Find the area of a triangle with base 16 cm and height 11 cm.
4. A circle has radius 7 inches. Find the circumference and area. (Use π ≈ 3.14.)
5. A floor plan is shaped like an L: a 10×8 rectangle with a 4×3 rectangle removed from one corner. Find the area.

**Answers:**
1. P = 2(9 + 5) = 28 m; A = 9 × 5 = **45 m²**
2. Side = 60 ÷ 4 = 15 ft; A = 15² = **225 ft²**
3. A = (1/2)(16)(11) = **88 cm²**
4. C = 2(3.14)(7) = **43.96 inches**; A = 3.14(7²) = 3.14(49) = **153.86 in²**
5. Full rectangle: 10 × 8 = 80; removed piece: 4 × 3 = 12; Area = 80 - 12 = **68 ft²**
