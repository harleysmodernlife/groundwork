## Percentages and Their Applications

A percentage is a ratio out of 100. The word comes from Latin: *per centum*, meaning "per hundred." When you say 40%, you mean 40 out of every 100 — or equivalently, 40/100, or 0.40. Percentages are the standard language for expressing proportions in business, science, and everyday life.

---

## Converting Between Percentages, Decimals, and Fractions

These three forms all express the same relationship. Being fluent in all three is essential.

### Percent to Decimal
Divide by 100, which means moving the decimal point two places to the left.
- 75% = 0.75
- 3% = 0.03
- 150% = 1.50

### Decimal to Percent
Multiply by 100 — move the decimal point two places to the right.
- 0.42 = 42%
- 0.08 = 8%
- 1.25 = 125%

### Percent to Fraction
Write the percentage over 100 and simplify.
- 25% = 25/100 = 1/4
- 60% = 60/100 = 3/5
- 37.5% = 37.5/100 = 375/1000 = 3/8

### Fraction to Percent
Divide numerator by denominator, then multiply by 100.
- 3/4 = 0.75 = 75%
- 2/3 = 0.6667 ≈ 66.7%
- 1/8 = 0.125 = 12.5%

## Finding a Percentage of a Number

"What is 30% of 80?"

**Method:** Convert the percent to a decimal and multiply.
- 30% = 0.30
- 0.30 × 80 = **24**

**Mental shortcut:** 10% of any number is just that number divided by 10. Then scale.
- 10% of 80 = 8
- 30% of 80 = 3 × 8 = **24**
- 5% of 80 = half of 10% = 4

This mental math method is fast enough to use in your head without paper.

---

## Finding What Percent One Number Is of Another

"What percent of 80 is 24?"

**Setup:** (part / whole) × 100 = percent
- (24 / 80) × 100 = 0.30 × 100 = **30%**

Another way to read this: divide the part by the whole, and the result (as a decimal) is the percentage.

---

## Finding the Original (Whole) When a Percentage Is Known

"24 is 30% of what number?"

**Setup:** part = percent × whole, so whole = part / percent
- whole = 24 / 0.30 = **80**

This type of question appears when you know the result of a percentage but need to find the original. For example: "A sale item costs $45, which is 75% of the original price. What was the original?"
- 45 / 0.75 = **$60**

## Percent Increase and Decrease

These measure how much something changed relative to where it started.

**Percent Increase:**
```
(New Value - Original Value) / Original Value × 100
```

**Example:** A salary goes from $50,000 to $56,000.
- Increase: 56,000 - 50,000 = 6,000
- 6,000 / 50,000 × 100 = **12% increase**

**Percent Decrease:**
```
(Original Value - New Value) / Original Value × 100
```

**Example:** Inventory drops from 200 units to 170 units.
- Decrease: 200 - 170 = 30
- 30 / 200 × 100 = **15% decrease**

## Applying Percentages: Discounts and Tax

### Calculating a Discount
An item costs $80 and is 25% off. How much do you pay?
- Method 1: Find discount amount and subtract. 25% of 80 = 20. Pay 80 - 20 = **$60**.
- Method 2: A 25% discount means you pay 75%. 0.75 × 80 = **$60**. (Faster once internalized.)

### Calculating Sales Tax
An item costs $120 and tax is 8.5%.
- Tax amount: 0.085 × 120 = $10.20
- Total: 120 + 10.20 = **$130.20**

Or: 108.5% of $120 = 1.085 × 120 = **$130.20**.

### Combining Discount and Tax
Item is $200, discounted 20%, then 8% tax.
1. Discounted price: 0.80 × 200 = $160
2. With tax: 1.08 × 160 = **$172.80**

**Important:** Apply the discount before the tax. Tax applies to the selling price, not the original price.

## Tip Calculations

A 20% tip on a $47 bill:
- 10% of 47 = 4.70
- 20% = 2 × 4.70 = **$9.40**

A 15% tip:
- 10% = 4.70
- 5% = 2.35
- 15% = 4.70 + 2.35 = **$7.05**

## Simple Interest

Simple interest calculates interest on the original principal only.

```
Interest = Principal × Rate × Time
```

Where rate is annual (per year) and time is in years.

**Example:** $5,000 at 6% annual interest for 3 years.
- I = 5,000 × 0.06 × 3 = **$900**
- Total owed: 5,000 + 900 = **$5,900**

## Practice

1. Convert 0.065 to a percent.
2. What is 35% of 240?
3. 18 is what percent of 72?
4. A jacket is $90, marked 40% off. What is the sale price?
5. A store applies 7% tax to the sale price from problem 4. What is the final amount?
6. A stock price went from $40 to $34. What is the percent decrease?

**Answers:**
1. 6.5%
2. 0.35 × 240 = **84**
3. 18/72 × 100 = **25%**
4. 0.60 × 90 = **$54**
5. 1.07 × 54 = **$57.78**
6. (40 - 34) / 40 × 100 = 6/40 × 100 = **15% decrease**
