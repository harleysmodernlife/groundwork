## Addition and Subtraction

Addition and subtraction are inverses of each other — they undo each other. Addition combines two or more quantities into a total. Subtraction finds the difference between two quantities, or removes one amount from another. These two operations are the first tools of arithmetic, and mastering them means understanding not just the procedure but the reasoning behind it.

---

## Addition: Combining Quantities

The **sum** is the result of adding. The numbers being added are called **addends**.

```
addend + addend = sum
   45  +   37  =  82
```

### The Standard Algorithm (Column Addition)

When adding multi-digit numbers, align the digits by place value and work from right to left:

```
   45
+  37
----
```

1. Ones column: 5 + 7 = 12. Write 2, carry the 1 to the tens column.
2. Tens column: 4 + 3 + 1 (carried) = 8. Write 8.
3. Result: **82**

**Carrying** (also called regrouping) happens whenever a column sums to 10 or more. The tens digit of that sum gets carried to the next column.

**Larger example:**
```
   489
+  376
-----
```
- Ones: 9 + 6 = 15. Write 5, carry 1.
- Tens: 8 + 7 + 1 = 16. Write 6, carry 1.
- Hundreds: 4 + 3 + 1 = 8. Write 8.
- Result: **865**

---

## Properties of Addition

**Commutative Property:** The order of addends doesn't affect the sum.
- 14 + 9 = 9 + 14 = 23

**Associative Property:** How you group addends doesn't affect the sum.
- (5 + 8) + 2 = 5 + (8 + 2) = 15

**Identity Property:** Adding zero leaves a number unchanged.
- 47 + 0 = 47

These properties matter because they let you rearrange additions to make mental math easier. Adding 97 + 46 is harder than adding (97 + 3) + 43 = 100 + 43 = 143.

## Subtraction: Finding the Difference

The **difference** is the result of subtracting. The number being subtracted from is the **minuend**; the number being subtracted is the **subtrahend**.

```
minuend - subtrahend = difference
    82  -     37    =    45
```

### The Standard Algorithm (Column Subtraction)

Align digits by place value and work right to left:

```
   82
-  37
----
```

1. Ones column: Can't subtract 7 from 2. **Borrow** from the tens place.
   - The 8 in the tens column becomes 7. The 2 in the ones column becomes 12.
   - 12 - 7 = 5. Write 5.
2. Tens column: 7 - 3 = 4. Write 4.
3. Result: **45**

**Borrowing** (regrouping) is the reverse of carrying. You take 10 from the next column left and add it to the current column.

**Larger example:**
```
   503
-  278
-----
```
- Ones: Can't do 3 - 8. Need to borrow from tens. Tens is 0, so borrow from hundreds.
  - Hundreds: 5 becomes 4. Tens: 0 becomes 10 (from hundreds borrow), then immediately lend 1 to ones: tens becomes 9, ones becomes 13.
  - 13 - 8 = 5. Write 5.
- Tens: 9 - 7 = 2. Write 2.
- Hundreds: 4 - 2 = 2. Write 2.
- Result: **225**

## Checking Your Work

Since addition and subtraction are inverses, you can check either operation using the other:
- If 72 - 45 = 27, then 27 + 45 should equal 72. ✓
- If 158 + 64 = 222, then 222 - 64 should equal 158. ✓

This is not just a classroom trick — it is a genuine error-detection method used in accounting, programming, and everyday calculation.

## Mental Math Strategies

### Making Tens
Add or subtract a round number, then adjust.
- 48 + 27: Think 48 + 30 = 78, then subtract 3 → **75**
- 83 - 29: Think 83 - 30 = 53, then add 1 → **54**

### Counting Up for Subtraction
Instead of subtracting, find how much to add to get from the smaller to the larger number.
- 91 - 67: Count up from 67 to 91. 67 + 3 = 70, then 70 + 21 = 91. Total added: 3 + 21 = **24**.

This technique is how cashiers make change without a calculator.

## Word Problems: Translating Language to Math

**Addition signals:** sum, total, altogether, combined, more than, in all
**Subtraction signals:** difference, less than, how many remain, how much more, lost, spent, removed

**Example:** A plumber charged $340 for parts and $215 for labor. What was the total bill?
- "Total" signals addition: 340 + 215 = **$555**

**Example:** A tank held 1,200 gallons. After draining 475 gallons, how much remains?
- "Remains after draining" signals subtraction: 1,200 - 475 = **725 gallons**
