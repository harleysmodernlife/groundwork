## 3D Solids: Surface Area and Volume

Three-dimensional geometry measures physical space — the space inside a container, the material needed to make a box, the paint required to cover a tank. Two key measurements apply to all 3D solids: **surface area** (the total area of all outer faces) and **volume** (the amount of space inside). These are not interchangeable — a shape's volume can be huge while its surface area is modest, and vice versa.

---

## Key Vocabulary

**Face:** A flat surface of a solid.
**Edge:** A line segment where two faces meet.
**Vertex:** A point where edges meet.
**Net:** A 2D pattern that folds up to form a 3D solid. Useful for calculating surface area.
**Lateral surface:** The side surfaces of a solid (excluding the bases).

---

## Prisms

A **prism** is a solid with two parallel, congruent bases connected by rectangular lateral faces. Prisms are named by the shape of their base: triangular prism, rectangular prism (box), hexagonal prism, etc.

**Volume of any prism:**
```
V = Base Area × Height
```

Where height is the perpendicular distance between the two bases.

**Surface Area of any prism:**
```
SA = 2 × Base Area + Lateral Surface Area
Lateral SA = Perimeter of base × Height
```

### Rectangular Prism (Box)

With length l, width w, height h:
- V = lwh
- SA = 2(lw + lh + wh)

**Example:** A box is 5 cm × 4 cm × 3 cm.
- V = 5 × 4 × 3 = **60 cm³**
- SA = 2(5×4 + 5×3 + 4×3) = 2(20 + 15 + 12) = 2(47) = **94 cm²**

### Triangular Prism

**Example:** A triangular prism with a right-triangle base (legs 3 and 4, hypotenuse 5) and length 10.
- Base area = (1/2)(3)(4) = 6
- Perimeter of base = 3 + 4 + 5 = 12
- V = 6 × 10 = **60 units³**
- SA = 2(6) + 12(10) = 12 + 120 = **132 units²**

---

## Cylinders

A **cylinder** has two circular bases connected by a curved lateral surface.

```
V = πr²h
SA = 2πr² + 2πrh = 2πr(r + h)
```

**Example:** A cylinder with radius 4 and height 10.
- V = π(4²)(10) = 160π ≈ **502.7 units³**
- SA = 2π(4)(4 + 10) = 8π(14) = 112π ≈ **351.9 units²**

**Real world:** A cylindrical can with diameter 8 cm and height 12 cm (radius = 4):
- Volume of liquid it holds: π(16)(12) = 192π ≈ **603.2 cm³**
- Metal needed (SA): 2π(4)(4 + 12) = 8π(16) = 128π ≈ **402.1 cm²**

---

## Pyramids

A **pyramid** has a polygonal base and triangular faces meeting at a single apex. Named by base shape.

```
V = (1/3) × Base Area × Height
```

The height is the perpendicular distance from the apex to the base.

**Lateral face area:** Each lateral face is a triangle. For a regular pyramid, all lateral faces are congruent isosceles triangles with height equal to the **slant height** (l) — the distance from the apex to the midpoint of a base edge.

```
Lateral SA = (1/2) × Perimeter of base × Slant Height
SA = Base Area + Lateral SA
```

**Example:** A square pyramid with base 6 cm, height 4 cm, slant height 5 cm.
- V = (1/3)(6²)(4) = (1/3)(36)(4) = **48 cm³**
- Lateral SA = (1/2)(24)(5) = **60 cm²**
- SA = 36 + 60 = **96 cm²**

---

## Cones

A **cone** has a circular base and a single apex, connected by a curved lateral surface.

```
V = (1/3)πr²h
```

**Slant height** (l) of a cone: the distance from the apex to the edge of the base circle. Found using the Pythagorean theorem: l = √(r² + h²).

```
Lateral SA = πrl
SA = πr² + πrl = πr(r + l)
```

**Example:** A cone with radius 3 and height 4.
- Slant height: l = √(9 + 16) = √25 = 5
- V = (1/3)π(9)(4) = 12π ≈ **37.7 units³**
- SA = π(3)(3 + 5) = π(3)(8) = 24π ≈ **75.4 units²**

---

## Spheres

A **sphere** is the set of all points equidistant from a center in three dimensions. There are no flat faces.

```
V = (4/3)πr³
SA = 4πr²
```

**Example:** A sphere with radius 6.
- V = (4/3)π(216) = 288π ≈ **904.8 units³**
- SA = 4π(36) = 144π ≈ **452.4 units²**

**Real world:** A spherical storage tank with radius 5 meters.
- Volume of liquid stored: (4/3)π(125) ≈ **523.6 m³**
- Paint needed (SA): 4π(25) ≈ **314.2 m²**

---

## Cross-Sections

A **cross-section** is the shape revealed when you slice a 3D solid with a plane.

- Slice a rectangular prism parallel to the base: rectangle.
- Slice a cylinder parallel to the base: circle.
- Slice a cylinder perpendicular to the base: rectangle.
- Slice a sphere through the center: circle (the largest possible, called a great circle).
- Slice a cone parallel to the base: circle. Slice at an angle: ellipse. Slice parallel to a side: parabola.

3. A pyramid has a square base with side 10 cm, height 12 cm, and slant height 13 cm. Find volume and surface area.
4. A cone has diameter 10 and height 12. Find the slant height, volume, and surface area.
5. A sphere has surface area 196π. Find its radius and volume.

4. r = 5, h = 12; l = √(25+144) = √169 = 13; V = (1/3)π(25)(12) = 100π ≈ **314.2 units³**; SA = π(5)(5+13) = 90π ≈ **282.7 units²**
